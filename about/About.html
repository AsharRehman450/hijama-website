About

Aafiyyah Hijama Center stands as a leading clinic in Karachi, Pakistan, renowned for its ISO certification, which ensures the highest standards of healthcare services and accredited training in Hijama Cupping Therapy.

Founded by Mohsin Malik, Aafiyyah Hijama Center represents a transformative vision of holistic healing. Mohsin, with his deep awareness of the unseen energies that influence our well-being, set out to transcend conventional healing methods. His exceptional healing abilities, combined with the understanding that human existence goes far beyond the physical body, became the cornerstone of this center. Aafiyyah Hijama Center is built on a commitment to promoting well-being by recognizing the intricate connection between mind, body, and spirit.

The center is devoted to providing top-tier healthcare services and certified training in Hijama Cupping Therapy. We also offer a range of therapeutic solutions through seminars and workshops, addressing a variety of physical, emotional, mental, and spiritual challenges. These educational sessions are designed to benefit individuals at any stage of life. Our philosophy is rooted in the belief that everyone deserves the opportunity to explore their spiritual potential in line with Islamic values. At Aafiyyah Hijama Center, we promote living in a space filled with pure, positive energy, fostering balance and serenity. The center is not only a place of healing but also a source of spiritual awakening, aiming to create healers who practice with humility. Our vision extends beyond Pakistan, striving to build a global community that embraces these principles.

Aafiyyah Hijama Center is much more than a clinic—it is a hub of holistic well-being and spiritual growth. Our mission extends beyond providing exceptional health services and certified Hijama training. At the heart of our approach is the belief that every person deserves the chance to reconnect with their spiritual essence, in accordance with Islamic teachings. We advocate for a life surrounded by clean, positive energy, encouraging harmony in every aspect of one's environment. Aafiyyah Hijama Center is not just a healthcare provider but a catalyst for personal transformation. We inspire individuals to embrace positive energy as the first step toward becoming compassionate and humble healers. Our goal is to expand this vision globally, forming communities worldwide that embody these values and contribute to a peaceful, enlightened world.
